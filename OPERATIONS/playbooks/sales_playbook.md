# Sales Playbook — Stratum AI

## 1. Prospecting (the ICP)
- **Clinic**: 2-10 chairs, busy front desk, >15% no-show, answering
  machine after hours. Title: Owner / Office Manager.
- **Brokerage**: 5-30 agents, portal + website leads, no CRM discipline.
  Title: Managing Broker.
- **Freight**: 20-200 trucks or 3PL doing 500+ invoices/month. Title:
  CFO / Ops Director / AP Manager.

## 2. First touch (email, see email_sequences/)
- One problem + one number + one ask. No attachments.
- Follow-up 1: offer the free mini-audit ("we'll run 50 of your real
  invoices/leads/calls through the system — you keep the findings").
- Follow-up 2 (last): soft-close, pause outreach.

## 3. Discovery call (20 min)
1. Their story: volumes, staff, what breaks. (10 min)
2. Metrics baseline: no-show %, response time, dispute backlog. (5 min)
3. Agree the proof-of-value test and success metric. (5 min)
4. Book the POV delivery call before hanging up.

## 4. Proof-of-value (the closer)
- Replay their data: missed calls / stale leads / historical invoices.
- Deliver a 1-page findings doc + live demo of the AI handling a
  comparable case.
- Show the ROI calculator filled with THEIR numbers.
- Ask for the decision: "If this were live, would you want it on?"

## 5. Pricing conversation
- Anchor on outcome value, then state the fee once (see pricing_strategy.md).
- Objections:
  - "Too expensive" → hours saved × hourly cost ≥ 2× fee, always show math.
  - "We'll build it ourselves" → 6-month timeline vs 2 weeks + your focus.
  - "AI isn't ready" → shadow mode pilot with zero risk, 14 days.
  - "No budget this quarter" → offer pilot at cost for a case study (max 2/yr).

## 6. Close & handoff
- SOW + NDA sent same day. Kickoff booked within 5 days.
- Hand the client to the onboarding manager with a one-pager: scope,
  contacts, promised metrics, quirks.

## 7. Expansion
- QBR at day 30/90: show metrics vs baseline, then pitch the next module
  (voice AI, extra locations, transaction assistant...).
- Ask for referrals at every QBR — vertical reference clients are gold.
