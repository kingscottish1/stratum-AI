# Hiring Plan (12 months)

| Quarter | Role | Trigger | Cost (annual, loaded) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Integration Engineer | First 2 clients signed | $95k |
| Q1 | Sales AE | Pipeline > 20 qualified | $80k + 10% commission |
| Q2 | AI Ops Specialist | 5+ live clients | $75k |
| Q3 | Account Manager | 10+ live clients | $70k + 5% of expansion |
| Q4 | Support Engineer L1 | Tickets > 20/wk | $60k |
| Q4 | Marketing fractional | CAC needs scaling | $40k |

## Sourcing
- Integration engineers: local talent pools (automation/IT background, trainable)
- Sales: vertical SaaS sellers preferred (healthcare/RE/freight SaaS background)
- AI Ops: prompt-engineering + ops; we train on the vertical playbooks

## Interview process (kept fast: 3 rounds max)
1. Intro + portfolio/work sample (30 min)
2. Technical/work simulation (60 min, paid exercise for senior roles)
3. Founder fit call (30 min)

## Onboarding
- Week 1: platform walkthrough, one vertical playbook, shadow support
- Week 2: paired integration work + certification checklist
- Week 3+: own small client task with review; monthly 1:1s
