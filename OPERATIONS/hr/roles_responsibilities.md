# Roles & Responsibilities

## Leadership
- **Founder/CEO** — strategy, key accounts, fundraising, final pricing calls
- **CTO/Head of Engineering** — platform architecture, reliability, security

## Delivery (the engine)
- **Onboarding/Integration Engineer** — client integrations, config,
  customizations; owns go-live timeline
- **AI Ops Specialist** — prompt tuning, replay analysis, accuracy reviews
- **Support Engineer (L1)** — triage, monitoring, first-response SLA

## Go-to-market
- **Sales/Account Executive** — pipeline, demos, POVs, pricing proposals
- **Account Manager (post-sale)** — QBRs, expansion, retention
- **Marketing (part-time/fractional)** — landing pages, case studies, outbound

## Finance & Ops (fractional until scale)
- Bookkeeper/CFO-as-a-service — invoicing, payroll, tax, P&L

## Hiring order (first 6 hires)
1. Integration Engineer (critical path to go-live)
2. Sales AE (fills the pipeline that pays for everything)
3. AI Ops Specialist (keeps accuracy at the 99% bar)
4. Account Manager (protects MRR once > 10 clients)
5. Support Engineer (when tickets > 20/week)
6. Marketing fractional (when CAC needs scaling)

## RACI highlights
- Pricing changes: CEO decides; AE proposes; AM implements
- Client copy changes: AI Ops + AM approve; integration engineer deploys
- New integrations: CTO gates; integration engineer builds; sales sells
