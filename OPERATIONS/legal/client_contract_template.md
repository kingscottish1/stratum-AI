# Client Services Agreement — Template (outline)

1. **Services** — implementation + platform per SOW (sow_template.docx),
   vertical package, support per SLA.
2. **Fees & payment** — implementation (50% on signature, 50% at go-live);
   platform monthly net 30; late fee 1.5%/mo; pause on 30+ days overdue.
3. **Term** — 12 months initial, then month-to-month, 30-day notice.
4. **Client data & privacy** — Agency processes as processor; DPA attached;
   security commitments; breach notification 72h.
5. **Compliance** — client warrants lawful data provision; HIPAA BAA where
   applicable; GDPR DPA where applicable.
6. **IP** — Agency owns platform/IP; client owns their data; client grants
   license to data needed to provide services.
7. **Confidentiality** — mutual, 3 years.
8. **Warranties & disclaimer** — services performed with professional care;
   no guarantee of specific outcomes; AI outputs reviewed with human
   oversight; third-party system availability excluded.
9. **Limitation of liability** — aggregate liability capped at 12 months
   of fees; no indirect/consequential damages.
10. **Termination** — convenience with 30 days; breach with 15-day cure;
    data export within 30 days of termination.
11. **Insurance** — professional liability & cyber coverage maintained.
12. **Governing law** — {{state/country}}; venue {{city}}.
