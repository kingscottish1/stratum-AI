# Pricing Tiers (internal)

## Tier structure
| Tier | Implementation | Platform/mo | Includes | Target vertical |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $3.5k – $4k | $750 – $1,000 | Core agents, 1 location/office/entity | Clinic / RE single office |
| Growth | $5k – $6k | $1,300 – $2,500 | Core + add-ons, up to 3 locations | Clinic / RE growth |
| Full Ops | $8k – $12k | $2,500 – $3,800 | Everything + advanced modules | Logistics |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Unlimited scale, dedicated SM | Any, 3+ locations |

## Rules of thumb
- Never price below a 50% projected margin without CEO sign-off.
- Implementation fee covers integrations + tuning; platform covers runtime,
  support and monitoring.
- Discounts: annual prepay = 2 months free; multi-site = 10-15%.
- POV (proof of value) is free and always offered — it is our best closer.

## When to raise prices
- Vertical reaches 5+ reference clients → +10-15% for new logos.
- Add-ons proven (e.g. voice AI) → package at premium, not bundle.
- Renewals: 5% annual escalation written into contract.
