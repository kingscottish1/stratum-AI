# Profit Margin Targets

## By vertical (gross margin on platform fees)
| Vertical | Target margin | Floor (never below) |
|---|---|---|
| Medical/Dental | 60% | 45% |
| Real Estate | 62% | 50% |
| Logistics | 62% | 55% |

## Company-level targets
- Blended gross margin: ≥ 58%
- Net margin (after G&A, sales, eng): ≥ 20% by month 18
- EBITDA-positive: month 15-18 at 40-50 clients
- Implementation fees: margin ≥ 40% (hours tracked per project)

## Levers if margins slip
1. LLM routing: ensure ≥ 70% of calls run on fast/cheap models
2. Support: move repetitive tickets to self-serve + playbooks
3. Infrastructure: right-size worker replicas; use spot for batch jobs
4. Pricing: grandfather old logos, raise for new ones

## Monthly review
Every 1st: finance reviews per-client P&L (cost_structure_by_vertical.xlsx),
flags clients below floor, proposes fix (price change / scope trim / churn).
