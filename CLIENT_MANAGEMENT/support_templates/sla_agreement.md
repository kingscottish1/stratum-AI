# Support SLA Agreement (template)

## Coverage
- Support hours: Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00 client timezone; 24/7 monitoring
- Channels: Slack #stratum-support, support@stratumai.com, phone (P1 only)

## Priorities & response targets
| Priority | Definition | First response | Fix/Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Platform down / data loss / booking system down | 30 min | 4 hours |
| P2 High | Major feature broken, workaround available | 2 hours | 24 hours |
| P3 Normal | Minor bug, cosmetic, single-user issue | 8 hours | 3 business days |
| P4 Low | Question, enhancement request | 24 hours | Next sprint |

## Availability
- Platform uptime target: 99.5% monthly (excluding scheduled maintenance)
- Scheduled maintenance: notified 7 days ahead, 02:00-04:00 client timezone

## Change requests
- Small changes (copy, config): included in platform fee, 5 business days
- New integrations/features: quoted per SOW amendment

## Reporting
- Weekly metrics email (Monday)
- Monthly business review call
- Quarterly roadmap review

## Out of scope
- Custom software development outside the vertical package
- On-site work (available at day rates)
- Third-party outages (carrier APIs, CRMs, etc.) — we monitor & alert, but
  uptime of third parties is their responsibility
