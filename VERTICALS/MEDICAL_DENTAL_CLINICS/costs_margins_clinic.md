# Clinic Vertical — Cost & Margin Model

## Positioning
Clinics buy *fewer no-shows + zero missed calls*. Price on outcomes, not hours.

## Target pricing
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Implementation (one-time) | $3,500 – $7,000 |
| Monthly platform (per location) | $750 – $1,800 |
| Add-ons: voice AI receptionist | +$500 – $1,000/mo |
| Add-ons: insurance intake automation | +$300 – $500/mo |
| Setup of extra channel (WhatsApp) | $250 one-time |

## Cost to serve (per client, per month)
| Cost item | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure (compute, DB, queue) | $60 – $120 |
| LLM tokens (typical 1,500 msgs/mo) | $40 – $90 |
| Twilio SMS/WhatsApp | $30 – $80 |
| Insurance verification API | $50 – $150 |
| Support & tuning (0.5-1 h/day) | $250 – $400 |
| **Total cost to serve** | **$430 – $840** |

## Margin math
- Entry client at $750/mo vs ~$500 cost → **~33% margin** (good for land)
- Standard client at $1,300/mo vs ~$600 cost → **~54% margin**
- Ideal client (3+ locations, add-ons) $4,000/mo vs ~$1,200 → **~70% margin**

## Unit economics targets
- Payback of implementation cost: ≤ 3 months (sells itself: 30 hrs/mo saved
  at $20/hr = $600/mo value + no-show reduction)
- LTV target: > 24 months × $1,300 = $31k
- CAC target: < $4k (SDR + demo) → LTV/CAC ≥ 7x

## What we sell (outcomes)
- "Save 25-40 front-desk hours/month"
- "Cut no-shows 40-60%"
- "Answer every call/message within 30 seconds, 24/7"
- "Insurance collected before the visit — fewer surprises"

## Expansion plays
- Additional locations (multiply MRR at 60-70% margin)
- Voice AI, billing follow-up, recall campaigns, review generation
- White-label for dental service organizations (DSOs) with 10+ locations
