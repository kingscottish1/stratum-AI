# Logistics Vertical — Cost & Margin Model

## Positioning
Freight companies run on paper and disputes. We sell *AP automation +
exception ops* — directly measurable money: disputes recovered, late fees
avoided, back-office hours returned.

## Target pricing
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Implementation (one-time, TMS+accounting+carriers) | $8,000 – $15,000 |
| Monthly platform (per client) | $2,000 – $4,500 |
| Per additional ERP/entity | +$500 – $1,000/mo |
| Add-on: carrier scorecard & negotiation pack | +$500 – $750/mo |
| Add-on: customer-facing shipment alerts | +$400 – $800/mo |

## Cost to serve (per client, per month)
| Cost item | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure (workers, OCR, storage) | $120 – $250 |
| LLM tokens (doc-heavy: 5k-15k docs/mo) | $150 – $400 |
| Email / document processing infra | $50 – $120 |
| Carrier API fees | $50 – $150 |
| Support & data tuning (1-2 h/day) | $450 – $700 |
| **Total cost to serve** | **$820 – $1,620** |

## Margin math
- Entry client at $2,000/mo vs ~$900 cost → **~55% margin**
- Standard at $3,200/mo vs ~$1,200 cost → **~62% margin**
- Enterprise (multi-entity + add-ons) $8,000/mo vs ~$2,800 → **~65% margin**

## Unit economics targets
- Implementation payback ≤ 3 months. Typical first-year value delivered:
  $15k-40k in disputes recovered + $20k-60k in back-office hours.
- LTV target: > 30 months × $3,200 = $96k (logistics clients churn slowly)
- CAC target: < $6k → LTV/CAC ≥ 12x

- "Disputes submitted in 24h instead of 6 weeks — recover the money"
