# Real Estate Vertical — Cost & Margin Model

## Positioning
Brokerages pay for *speed-to-lead and showings booked*. They measure
everything in pipeline value, so price in that language.

## Target pricing
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Implementation (one-time) | $4,000 – $8,000 |
| Monthly platform (per office, ≤ 10 agents) | $1,000 – $2,500 |
| Per extra agent seat | $75 – $150/mo |
| Add-on: AI voice outreach (DNC-compliant) | +$600 – $1,200/mo |
| Add-on: transaction/closing assistant | +$400 – $800/mo |

## Cost to serve (per client, per month)
| Cost item | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure | $60 – $120 |
| LLM tokens (typ. 2,000 msgs/mo + matching) | $50 – $110 |
| Twilio + email | $40 – $90 |
| MLS/RESO API fees | $50 – $150 |
| CRM fees (if we pay; usually client) | $0 – $100 |
| Support & tuning | $300 – $450 |
| **Total cost to serve** | **$500 – $1,020** |

## Margin math
- Small office at $1,000/mo vs ~$550 cost → **~45% margin**
- Standard at $2,000/mo vs ~$750 cost → **~62% margin**
- Large (5+ offices, add-ons) $8,000/mo vs ~$2,500 → **~69% margin**

## Unit economics targets
- Implementation payback ≤ 3 months (a single extra closed deal ≈ 1 year of fees)
- LTV target: > 24 months × $2,000 = $48k
- CAC target: < $4.5k → LTV/CAC ≥ 10x (best-in-class SaaS)

## What we sell (outcomes)
- "Every lead answered in under 5 minutes — even at 2am"
- "X showings booked per week without touching your agents' phones"
- "Never lose a lead to slow follow-up again"
- "Your agents sell; the AI runs the chase"

## Expansion plays
- Add offices/agents (per-seat pricing)
- Listing-side automation (valuation reports, listing syndication QA)
- Transaction coordination add-on (forms, milestones, reminders)
- Franchise/team play: sell to regional teams, standardize onboarding
