# Client Dashboard Guide

You get a weekly email + live dashboard with these metrics.

## Speed
- **Time-to-first-response** (target < 5 min hot, < 60 min all)
- **Coverage %** — share of inbound handled by AI (nights/weekends = 100%)

## Qualification
- Leads scored / total inbound
- Tier distribution (hot/warm/cold)
- Score accuracy vs agent feedback (weekly calibration)

## Activity
- Showings booked by AI
- Viewing confirmations / no-shows
- Sequence replies & opt-outs
- Deals where AI touched the process (offer → closing)

## Business impact (the ones that matter)
- **Pipeline value of AI-handled leads** (est. commission at 2.8%)
- **Hours saved** (est. 2-3 min per AI-handled interaction)
- **Conversion lift** vs pre-AI baseline (measured after 60 days)

## Access
- Dashboard URL: provided at go-live (Grafana + client portal)
- Weekly PDF: emailed every Monday 8am
- Alerts: Slack channel for anomalies (e.g. reply rate drop)
