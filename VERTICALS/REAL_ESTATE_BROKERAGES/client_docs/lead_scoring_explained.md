# Lead Scoring — Explained

Every lead gets a 0-100 score in seconds. Here's exactly what drives it.

## Weights (v1, calibrated per brokerage)
| Signal | Points (max) |
|---|---|
| Intent (buy=30, sell=25, invest=20, rent=10) | 30 |
| Timeline (asap=25, 1-3mo=20, 3-6mo=15, 6mo+=8) | 25 |
| Financing (cash=30, preapproved=25, mortgage=10) | 30 |
| Has area/preference | 10 |
| Fast channel (phone/SMS) | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Tiers
- **Hot (70-100)** — agent alerted instantly; property matching + showing
  offer triggered automatically; aim: contact < 5 min.
- **Warm (40-69)** — AI replies immediately, enters nurture sequence,
  receives new-listing alerts.
- **Cold (0-39)** — monthly market snapshot drip; re-scored every 30 days.

## How we calibrate
Every week the managing broker reviews a sample: "what did this lead
actually do?" Scores are re-weighted so the model improves monthly.
Your team can override any lead's tier with one click in the CRM.

## Why it matters
Typical brokerage: 70% of leads are never followed up within 5 minutes,
and follow-up speed is the #1 conversion lever (NAR studies). Our system
guarantees < 5 min for hot leads, 24/7.
