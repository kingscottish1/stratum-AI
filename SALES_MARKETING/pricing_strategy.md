# Pricing Strategy

## Principles
1. **Price on outcomes, not hours.** Clients compare us to staff cost —
   a front-desk hire costs $35-50k/yr. We must cost less and deliver more.
2. **Implementation + monthly platform** (SaaS-style): predictable revenue,
   compounding MRR.
3. **Land small, expand fast.** Entry pricing is deliberately affordable;
   margin grows with add-ons and locations/entities.
4. **Proof-of-value before close.** The free mini-audit (replay their real
   data) is the #1 conversion tool — it converts at ~3x vs no-POV.

## The pricing model
```
Implementation fee (one-time)
  clinic:      $3,500 – $7,000
  real estate: $4,000 – $8,000
  logistics:   $8,000 – $15,000

Platform fee (monthly, per client instance)
  clinic:      $750 – $1,800  (+$500-1,000 per extra location)
  real estate: $1,000 – $2,500 (+$75-150 per extra agent seat)
  logistics:   $2,000 – $4,500 (+$500-1,000 per extra entity)

Add-ons: voice AI, extra channels, advanced modules ($300 – $1,200/mo)
```

## Discounting rules
- Annual prepay: 2 months free (improves cash flow, reduces churn).
- Multi-location/entity deals: 10-15% discount on the total.
- Never discount below 50% margin. Never discount implementation for
  logos unless it's a flagship case study (max 2/year, C-suite approved).

## Packaging for each vertical (what the price card says)
- **Clinic**: "AI front desk" — booking, reminders, insurance intake, follow-up.
- **Real estate**: "AI lead pipeline" — qualification, matching, viewings, nurture.
- **Logistics**: "AP & exception automation" — invoice matching, disputes, alerts, reports.

## Renewal & expansion levers
- QBR (quarterly business review) with ROI numbers → expansion trigger.
- Product-led expansion: new modules are priced and sold to warm base.
- Annual escalation: 5% CPI-style increase on platform fee at renewal.

## Metrics we track
- MRR, ARR, gross margin per vertical, LTV, CAC, payback months,
  churn (target < 1.5%/mo), expansion revenue, POV → close conversion.
