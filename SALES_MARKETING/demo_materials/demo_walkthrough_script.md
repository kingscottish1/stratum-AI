# Demo Walkthrough Script (15 min)

## 0:00 — Hook (2 min)
"Every minute a lead waits, someone else answers it. This demo shows how
we take that minute to zero — for [vertical]."

## 0:02 — The problem (2 min)
- Show the client's actual pain: missed calls report, no-show rate,
  invoice dispute backlog (use their numbers from discovery).
- "None of this is a people problem. It's a systems problem."

## 0:04 — Live demo: the agent (6 min)
1. Send a real message to the demo number: "Hi, can I book a
   [appointment/viewing/—] this week?"
2. Watch the flow: classification → availability → booking → confirmation
   in under 15 seconds.
3. Test the edge: "Actually I need to cancel" → handled instantly.
4. Test insurance/qualification: show data captured, CRM record updated.
5. If logistics: show invoice match — clean match pays, discrepancy holds
   with evidence.

## 0:10 — The numbers (3 min)
- Show ROI calculator: hours saved, no-show reduction, disputes recovered.
- Implementation timeline: 2-4 weeks. Pricing: transparent, one page.

## 0:13 — Close (2 min)
- "Next step: we run a free mini-audit / proof-of-value on your real data."
- Book follow-up in calendar immediately. Ask for decision-maker access.

## Scripted one-liners
- "The AI doesn't replace your team — it absorbs the 80% that's repetitive."
- "You approve everything. We just make approval the only step left."
- "If it doesn't pay for itself in month one, that's our problem."
