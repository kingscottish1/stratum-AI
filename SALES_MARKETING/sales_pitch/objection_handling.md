# Stratum AI — Objection Handling

> Every objection, answered. Built by kingscottishDEV · N.A.S — Nexus Audit Security.

## 1. "It's too expensive"
"Let's put it next to what it replaces. You told me {their number} —
that's ~${value} a month in {missed bookings / cold leads / overpaid
invoices}. The platform is $X. It pays for itself in month one, and we
prove it with the free mini-audit before you spend anything. If the
audit doesn't show at least 2× the fee in value, don't buy."

## 2. "We'll build it ourselves"
"Honestly, that's the right instinct — and the wrong math. A competent
build is 6-12 months of engineering plus integrations that fight back
(Dentrix/MLS/TMW APIs aren't fun), plus you own the maintenance. We're
live in 2-4 weeks, tuned on your data, with support. Your time is worth
more selling/fixing patients/deals/invoices than debugging webhooks."

## 3. "AI isn't reliable / what if it books something wrong?"
"Fair — so we run it in shadow mode first: the AI does the work, your
team approves everything, nothing happens without a human for the first
48 hours. After that, every action is logged, one click to undo, and
you can keep any workflow in confirm-first forever. In production runs,
false bookings are under 1% — and you control the switch."

## 4. "HIPAA / compliance / our data"
"We're built for it: encryption at rest (AES-256-GCM), JWT auth, full
audit trails, per-client encrypted secrets, no hardcoded keys, and we
sign BAAs/DPAs. Your data is yours — we don't train models on it, and
you can bring your own LLM so even the brain is yours."

## 5. "My staff will resist / they don't like new tools"
"The AI doesn't replace them — it absorbs the repetitive 80% they hate
(cold calls, chasing paperwork, reminder calls). Your front desk gets
the interesting 20%. And they don't operate it; it works for them. We
train them in one session."

## 6. "We tried automation before and it failed"
"What usually fails is generic software that doesn't understand the
industry — it can't book into your PMS, read your invoices, or match
your leads. We're vertical-native: built for {clinics/brokerages/
freight}, wired to your actual stack, tuned on your actual data. That's
why the free mini-audit exists — let the data decide, not the hype."

## 7. "Not now — it's busy season / we're short-staffed"
"That's exactly when it pays for itself fastest. And honestly, the less
staff you have, the more calls/leads/invoices slip. We can be live in
shadow mode in two weeks with zero disruption — the busiest time is the
best time to test it."

## 8. "What if it breaks and we're stuck?"
"You get support with SLAs (P1 response in 30 min), monitoring on our
side, and every action is logged so nothing is ever lost. Third-party
outages (Twilio, your PMS) we detect and alert. And you can flip any
workflow back to manual with one click — the system degrades to your
current process, never below it."

## 9. "Can you prove it works for someone like us?"
"We'll run your real data through the system free — 50 invoices, your
last 100 leads, or a week of missed calls. You keep the findings. Then
we'll connect you with a reference in your vertical (we'll pick the
closest fit to your size)."

## 10. "I need to think about it"
"Of course. Two things that make the decision easy: the mini-audit is
free and yours either way, and we can hold the implementation slot.
When would it be fair to follow up — Thursday or Friday?"

## Never say
- "The AI is basically perfect" (it isn't; human oversight is the feature)
- "Your competitors are already using it" (pressure without proof)
- "Just trust us" (we show data instead)
- Anything about specific client names without written permission
