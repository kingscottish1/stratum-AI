## 0:00-0:02 — Open (why this call exists)
"Thanks for the time, {name}. Quick context: we build AI operations for
{their industry} — and specifically we take the repetitive work that
loses you money and run it automatically: {one-line for vertical}. I'd
like to show you how in about 15 minutes, but first — what's the
biggest pain in {front desk / lead follow-up / AP} right now?"

## 0:02-0:05 — Discovery (listen 80% of this block)
Ask, in order:
1. "How many {calls/leads/invoices} do you get in a typical {day/week/month}?"
2. "What happens to them when you're busy or closed?"
3. "What does that cost you, roughly? (Do you know your {no-show
   rate / 5-minute response rate / overpayment rate}?)"
4. "What have you tried? (hired someone? CRM? chatbot?) What happened?"
5. "If we fixed {their #1 pain} this quarter, what would that be worth?"

Lock the number. You will use it to close.

## 0:05-0:12 — The pitch (their vertical)
### Stratum Care
"Here's what we do. Stratum Care is an AI front desk:
- Every call, text and webchat answered in under 30 seconds — including
  at lunch, at night, weekends. It books, reschedules and cancels
  straight into your {calendar/PMS}.
- Before the visit it collects insurance details conversationally and
  verifies coverage — no more chasing cards at the front desk.
- Reminders at 48h, 24h and 2h cut no-shows dramatically. One practice
  went from 21% to 9% in 60 days.
- If a patient needs a human, it hands off to your front desk with the
  full conversation — you approve everything in shadow mode first.

For you specifically: you said {their number} — that's about ${estimate}
a month in {missed bookings / no-shows}. We'd cut it by roughly half.
Two weeks to go live. You approve the copy, we tune it on your real
conversations first."

### Stratum Realty
"Stratum Realty is a lead pipeline that never sleeps:
- Every lead from your website and portals is scored 0-100 in seconds —
  intent, budget, timeline, financing.
- Hot leads alert the right agent in under 5 minutes — even at 2am. You
  said {their number} leads/month; at a 5-minute response that converts
  measurably better (it's the #1 lever in real estate).
- It books viewings on agent calendars with confirmations to both sides,
  sends 3-5 matched listings from the MLS, and runs nurture sequences so
  warm leads stay warm.
- One Denver brokerage: 486 leads scored, 214 showings booked in a
  single month, no extra headcount.

Your math: if one extra deal closes this year, that's ~$10-15k — this
pays for itself before the quarter ends. And it runs in shadow first, so
nothing happens without your team's approval."

### Stratum Freight
"Stratum Freight is AP and exception automation:
- Every carrier invoice is parsed and matched against the contracted
  rate and POD before payment. Clean invoices pay; discrepancies hold
  with the evidence attached — you said {their number} invoices/month,
  and 1-3% leakage on that is ${estimate}/year.
- Disputes are packaged with evidence and filed in 24 hours instead of
  six weeks — when they're still winnable.
- It watches every shipment hourly: scan gaps, late risks, missing PODs
  — and chases the paperwork automatically. Exceptions surface before
  customers call, not after.
- One client recovered $38k in year one and gave AP 30 hours a month back.

We'll start by running 50 of your real invoices through it — free. You
keep the findings either way."

## 0:12-0:15 — Trust, security, how it deploys
"How do we do it safely? Three steps:
- **Connect** — we plug into what you already use (list their stack).
- **Tune** — we replay your real data; the AI matches your business.
- **Shadow → live** — for the first 48h the AI works and your team
  approves everything. You control every switch.

On security: credentials are encrypted at rest, every action is logged
to an audit trail, nothing is hardcoded, we sign a DPA/BAA where needed,
and you bring your own AI model if you prefer — no lock-in."
