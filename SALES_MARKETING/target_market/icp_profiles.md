## ICP 1 — STRATUM CARE: Independent & small-group clinics

**Who:** Independent dental practices and medical clinics (2-10 chairs /
exam rooms), 1-4 locations, owner-operated or small DSO/group member.
5,000-50,000 patients.

**Firmographics**
- 1-3 front-desk staff (often one), no dedicated marketing or IT
- Runs on Dentrix / EagleSoft / Open Dental / NexHealth-style stack
- Phone is the #1 booking channel; website may have a form
- 10-60 appointments/day per location

**Jobs-to-be-done**
- Never miss a booking opportunity (calls, texts, web)
- Fill the schedule, especially hygiene/recall
- Get insurance right before the visit
- Stop the front desk from burning out

**Pains (quantified)**
- 1 in 3 inbound calls hits voicemail (industry studies) — each missed
  booking ≈ $200-600 lifetime value
- No-shows 15-25% at a cost of $9k-15k/mo per location
- 15-30 min/day per staff member chasing insurance details
- Front-desk turnover: hiring + training costs $5-15k per event

**Budget & willingness to pay**
- $750-1,800/mo platform + $3.5-7k implementation
- ROI math that closes: 30 hrs/mo saved ≈ $600-900 + no-show recovery
- Pays for itself if it saves ~3 front-desk hours a day

**Decision maker & influencers**
- Owner/dentist (economic buyer), Office Manager (champion/user),
  sometimes DSO regional manager
- Average deal cycle: 2-6 weeks after POV

**Buying triggers**
- Front-desk staff quit / on leave; no-show spike; new location opening;
  competitor offering online booking; after-hours complaint from a patient

**Where they hang out**
- ADA / local dental society meetings, dental Facebook groups, DSO
  conferences, dental supplier ecosystems (Henry Schein, Patterson),
  dental marketing podcasts/newsletters

## ICP 2 — STRATUM REALTY: Independent brokerages & teams

**Who:** Independent brokerages and top-producing teams, 3-30 agents,
one managing broker + maybe one ISA. Lead sources: website, Zillow/
Realtor portals, referrals.

**Firmographics**
- Runs Follow Up Boss / kvCORE / LionDesk (or nothing organized)
- 10-100 leads/month, mostly portal + website
- Agents do everything: showings, listings, follow-up (and hate the follow-up)
- 1-5 offices

**Jobs-to-be-done**
- Contact every lead fast (speed-to-lead is the #1 conversion lever)
- Keep the pipeline warm without nagging agents
- Protect the pipeline when agents leave
- Convert portal spend into appointments

**Pains (quantified)**
- ~70% of leads never contacted within 5 minutes; most go cold
- One lost deal ≈ $10-15k commission — pays for a year of the platform
- Hiring an ISA costs $40-60k/yr; they burn out in ~18 months
- Weekends/holidays = dead zone for lead response

**Budget & willingness to pay**
- $1,000-2,500/mo + $4-8k implementation; $75-150/mo per extra agent seat
- ROI math that closes: 1 extra closing/year pays the whole year

**Decision maker & influencers**
- Managing broker/owner (economic buyer); top agents (influencers —
  they hate follow-up, love booked showings)

**Buying triggers**
- Portal spend rising without conversions; open-house season; losing a
  deal to a faster competitor; agent complaints about lead volume;
  ISA leaving; growth without adding staff

**Where they hang out**
- Local NAR/board meetings, brokerage owner communities (Tom Ferry,
  Mike Ferry ecosystems), real-estate Facebook groups, Inman events,
  portal partner summits (Zillow Premier Agent events)

## ICP 3 — STRATUM FREIGHT: Brokers, 3PLs & fleet back offices

**Who:** Freight brokerages, 3PLs and mid-size fleets with in-house
operations, processing 500-5,000 carrier invoices/month.

**Firmographics**
- 1-5 AP/back-office staff; ops team 5-50
- TMS: TMW / 4Front / Logistic Manager / McLeod; Accounting: QuickBooks,
  Sage Intacct or NetSuite
- Documents arrive by email, EDI, fax, portals
- 20-200 trucks or a brokerage book of business

**Jobs-to-be-done**
- Pay carriers right (contract compliance) and on time (no late fees)
- Recover overcharges/disputes while they're still winnable
- Catch exceptions before customers complain
- Keep AP/back-office headcount flat as volume grows

**Pains (quantified)**
- 1-3% of freight spend leaks to overcharges & unauthorized accessorials
  (on $2-10M/yr freight = $20k-300k/yr)
- Disputes filed after the recovery window = money lost permanently
- POD chasing eats 10+ hours/week of AP time
- Exceptions surface when a customer calls, not before

**Budget & willingness to pay**
- $2,000-4,500/mo + $8-15k implementation; $500-1,000 per extra entity
- ROI math that closes: $15-40k/yr disputes recovered + 30 hrs/mo AP time

**Decision maker & influencers**
- CFO/Controller (economic buyer), AP Manager (champion), Ops Director
  (influencer); brokers/3PL owners in smaller shops

**Buying triggers**
- Annual audit or CFO cost review; carrier rate increases; AP backlog;
- scaling volume without hiring; failed/disputed load incidents;
- new ERP/TMS rollout

**Where they hang out**
- TIA (Transportation Intermediaries Association) events, freight broker
  forums & Facebook groups, DAT/Truckstop ecosystems, accounting
  automation conferences, industry newsletters (FreightWaves etc.)

## Qualification checklist (use on every discovery call)
- [ ] Matches firmographics for one ICP?
- [ ] Named pain with an owner's number attached (no-show %, leads/mo, invoices/mo)?
- [ ] Decision maker in the room / identified?
- [ ] Budget band fits our pricing (or value clearly > 2× fee)?
- [ ] Willing to share real data for the free mini-audit?
  → 5/5 yes = pursue hard. 3-4 = nurture. <3 = not now.
