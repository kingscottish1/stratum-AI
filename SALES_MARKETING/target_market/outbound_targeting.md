# Stratum AI — Outbound Targeting Playbook

> How to find and reach the ICPs. Built by kingscottishDEV · N.A.S — Nexus Audit Security

## Where to get the lists
| Vertical | List sources | Titles to target |
|---|---|---|
| Care | Dentist/physician directories, ADA member lists, DSO lists, local business data (ZoomInfo, Apollo), supplier partners (Henry Schein/Patterson resellers) | Owner, DDS, Office Manager, Practice Administrator |
| Realty | Local board rosters, brokerage websites, portal advertiser lists, CRM vendors' public customers | Managing Broker, Team Lead, Operations Manager, ISA |
| Freight | FMCSA/TIA member directories, freight broker lists (DAT), 3PL directories, ERP partners | CFO, Controller, AP Manager, Ops Director, Owner |

## Multi-channel cadence (per prospect)
1. **Day 0** — personalized cold email (see SALES_MARKETING/email_sequences/) with ONE number for their industry
2. **Day 3** — follow-up #1: offer the free mini-audit ("50 of your real
   invoices/leads/calls — you keep the findings")
3. **Day 6** — LinkedIn connection + short note (no pitch, one insight)
4. **Day 9** — follow-up #2: soft close + case study link
5. **Day 12** — phone call (if number available), else stop; re-enter in 60 days

## Weekly targets (per SDR/AE)
- 100-150 new prospects added · 300-400 emails · 30-50 calls ·
  15-25 LinkedIn touches
- Expected: 3-5% reply, 1-2% demo booked, 0.5-1% POV started
- Demo → POV → close: ~25-30% at each stage

## Event & partnership channel
- Care: state dental association meetings, DSO summits — booth or speak
  on "no-show economics"
- Realty: local board trainings, brokerage owner masterminds — teach
  "speed-to-lead math"
- Freight: TIA events, freight broker roundtables — workshop on
  "auditing your carrier invoices in a week"
- Partnerships: CRM/stack vendors (referral 10-15% first-year fee),
  accountants who serve clinics/brokerages/freight

## Qualification gate (only book demos for these)
1. Named pain with a number attached
2. Decision maker identified
3. Can provide real data for the mini-audit
4. Budget sanity: value we can show ≥ 2× the fee
5. Timeline: "fixing this in the next 90 days" = yes

## 90-day plan summary
- **Month 1:** pick ONE vertical, 500-800 prospects, perfect the
  mini-audit offer, 5-10 demos, 2-3 POVs → 1-2 pilots
- **Month 2:** add second vertical, convert pilots to case studies,
  build referral asks into every QBR
- **Month 3:** third vertical + expansion plays on the installed base;
  start writing the first vertical playbook lock (see OPERATIONS/playbooks)
