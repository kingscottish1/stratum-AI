# Stratum AI — Market Sizing (planning estimates)

> Rough order-of-magnitude planning numbers. **Verify with current
> industry sources before investor use.** All $ = USD, per year.

## STRATUM CARE
- US dental practices: ~185k (ADA) · US physician/medical practices
  (small, independent): ~150-250k
- **TAM**: ~350k practices × $1.3k/mo avg platform ≈ **$5.5B/yr**
- **SAM** (2+ front-desk staff, digital-ready, 2-10 chairs): ~60-80k
  practices × $1.3k/mo ≈ **$1.1B/yr**
- **SOM** (5 years, realistic with 0.5-1% penetration): 400-800 clients
  ≈ **$6-12M ARR**

## STRATUM REALTY
- US brokerages/teams: ~100k (NAR local boards membership ~1.5M agents,
  most in small teams); target = teams with 3-30 agents ≈ 25-40k
- **TAM**: ~100k × $1.8k/mo ≈ **$2.2B/yr**
- **SAM**: 25-40k target teams × $1.8k/mo ≈ **$600-900M/yr**
- **SOM** (5 years): 300-600 clients ≈ **$6-14M ARR**

## STRATUM FREIGHT
- Active freight brokers ~20-30k (FMCSA/TIA estimates) + 3PLs + mid-size
  fleets with in-house ops ≈ 40-60k companies
- **TAM**: 50k × $3.2k/mo ≈ **$1.9B/yr**
- **SAM** (500+ invoices/mo, TMS/accounting ready): 10-15k companies
  ≈ **$400-600M/yr**
- **SOM** (5 years): 150-300 clients ≈ **$6-12M ARR**

## Blended

## Notes
- Cross-sell/expansion (voice AI, extra locations, seats, entities,
  transaction assistant) adds 20-40% to ARPU over time — not included above.
- Adjacent verticals later (legal intake, auto shops, property mgmt)
  double the TAM — deliberately out of scope for now (focus > breadth).
