| Product 3 | **Stratum Freight** | Logistics & freight |

## Name meaning
*Stratum* (plural *strata*): a distinct layer — a platform built in layers
(data, agent, operations) that is structured, foundational and
enterprise-grade. Institutional weight that business buyers trust, and
differentiation from overused tech names.

Rules: clear space = height of the "S" on all sides; never stretch,
rotate, recolor, or place on busy photography without a scrim; use the
icon alone only at small sizes (favicon, avatar).

## Voice & taglines
Professional, warm, outcome-driven — numbers, not hype. Approved taglines:
- Stratum AI — "Operations, automated layer by layer."
- Stratum Care — "Your front desk never misses another call — or booking."
- Stratum Realty — "Every lead answered in under 5 minutes — even at 2am."
- Stratum Freight — "Stop overpaying carriers — and chasing paperwork."

## Do / Don't
- DO pair every claim with a number (case studies have real metrics).
- DO use teal as an accent, not wallpaper.
- DON'T use "agency" alone as the brand; DON'T claim "AI magic" —
  always: outcomes, human approval, compliance, speed to value.
- Full messaging: `SALES_MARKETING/brand/positioning.md` and
  `messaging_house.md`. Social specs: `social_kit.md`.
