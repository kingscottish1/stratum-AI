> Built by kingscottishDEV · N.A.S — Nexus Audit Security

## Positioning statement
For **owner-operated medical/dental practices, real estate brokerages and
freight companies** who are losing money to missed calls, slow follow-up
and unchecked invoices, **Stratum AI** is an **AI operations platform** that
**runs the repetitive 80% of front-desk, lead and back-office work —
24/7, automatically**. Unlike **generic CRMs, chatbots, call centers or
hiring more staff**, Stratum AI **plugs into the tools they already use
(Dentrix, MLS, TMW, QuickBooks…), goes live in 2-4 weeks, and pays for
itself in month one**.

## Category
We are **not** a chatbot company, an RPA vendor, or a CRM. We are a
**vertical AI operations platform**: software that *does the work*, not
just tracks it, for three specific industries we know deeply.

## What the buyer is doing today (the alternatives)
| Alternative | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Hire more staff | $35-50k/yr per hire, never enough at peak hours, high turnover |
| Generic CRM / helpdesk | Tracks the work — doesn't do it. Still needs humans to book, follow up, match invoices |
| Chatbots | Answer questions; can't book into a calendar, verify insurance, hold a disputed invoice |
| Outsourced call center | $1,500-3k/mo per seat, scripted, not integrated with their systems |
| Doing nothing | The leakage compounds: missed calls → lost patients; slow leads → lost deals; late disputes → lost money |

## Why Stratum AI wins
1. **It does the work** — books, qualifies, matches, disputes. Not just "tracks".
2. **Vertical-native out of the box** — real integrations (Dentrix, EagleSoft, Follow Up Boss, RESO/MLS, TMW, QuickBooks, FedEx/UPS/DHL, Twilio…).
3. **Outcome-priced** — flat fee, pays for itself in month one, measured in hours saved and revenue recovered.
4. **Zero-risk adoption** — shadow mode first (AI recommends, humans approve), then live.
5. **Bring your own LLM** — no vendor lock-in; your data stays yours; plug in OpenAI, Anthropic, Azure, Groq, Ollama or any endpoint.
6. **Enterprise-grade security** — AES-256-GCM encryption at rest, JWT auth, full audit trails, no hardcoded secrets anywhere.
