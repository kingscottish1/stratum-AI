## Content pillars (post ~3-4x/week, rotate)
1. **Proof** (40%) — outcome stories: "312 appointments booked by AI in
   one month" with a short case caption. Use real numbers, anonymized.
2. **Education** (30%) — one operational insight per industry:
   "1 in 3 calls to a clinic goes to voicemail. Here's what that costs
   in a year." (formula + number)
3. **Product** (15%) — short demo clips (screen recording of an agent
   booking / matching an invoice / qualifying a lead), captions with the
   headline + CTA.
4. **Culture/trust** (15%) — security (encrypted at rest, audit trails),
   built-by story (kingscottishDEV · N.A.S), behind-the-scenes.
